SLEEP TIMER PRO
===============

1. Open this zip file (you're already here!)

2. Drag "SleepTimerPro.exe" to your Desktop

3. Double-click it to run

---------------------------------
If Windows shows a warning saying
"Windows protected your PC":

  → Click "More info"
  → Click "Run anyway"

This is normal for small apps that
aren't from a big company store.
---------------------------------

HOW TO USE THE APP:
- Set the number of minutes
- Choose Sleep (recommended) or Shutdown
- Press Start Timer
- Use "To Tray" to hide it while you
  listen to music or podcasts

The app will put your PC to sleep
when the timer runs out.

Enjoy!
